# Blockchain Market Overview

The blockchain landscape has evolved significantly over the past few years. We are now seeing mature DeFi protocols, robust L2 infrastructure, and massive adoption of specific use cases like Telegram Mini Apps.

## Key Trends

- **L2 Dominance**: Ethereum L2s have taken over the majority of transaction volume.
- **DeFi Maturation**: Institutional adoption of DeFi is bridging the gap between TradFi and Web3.
- **Micro-apps**: Platforms like Telegram and Farcaster are driving mass consumer adoption of crypto through embedded wallets and mini-apps.

## Conclusion

The infrastructure phase is largely behind us. The market now rewards actual end-user applications that solve real problems.
